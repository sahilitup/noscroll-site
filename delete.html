Delete your No Scroll data

No Scroll: Reel Counter is made by Sahil Satish Singh. The app has no logins. The only account it creates is an anonymous Scroll Battles profile, and only if you choose to join Battles. This page explains how to delete that profile and its data.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open No Scroll and go to the Battles tab.
  2. Tap Leave Battles and delete my data.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

This happens straight away.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email sahilsingh1601@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my No Scroll data". Tell us your Battles nickname and, if you know it, your 6-letter friend code. We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous account ID
  • Your nickname and friend code
  • Your friend list, and your entry on your friends' lists
  • Your daily reel totals

We don't keep any of it after deletion. Your reel counts and settings on your phone never reach our servers. Uninstalling the app removes them.
