Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

No Scroll ("we", "the app") helps you track and limit the short-form videos you watch. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Accessibility Service

No Scroll uses Android's Accessibility Service API for one purpose: to detect when you swipe to a new reel or short inside Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and Snapchat, so it can count them and, if you set a limit, close the reels feed when you reach it. To show the live counter only while those apps are open, it also notes the package name of whichever app is in front; it reads nothing else from other apps. It does not read your messages, what you type, passwords, contacts, or the content of any video, and none of this leaves your phone.

Data stored on your device

  • Your daily reel count for each app.
  • Your limit and focus-lock settings.

This data stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data stored on our servers (only if you join Scroll Battles)

  • An anonymous account ID (Firebase Authentication).
  • The nickname you choose, your friend code, the IDs of the friends you add, and your total reel count for the day.

Other No Scroll players can see your nickname and daily total. We store this data with Google Firebase. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

To delete your Battles data, open the Battles tab and tap Leave Battles and delete my data. This deletes your nickname, friend code, friend list and daily totals from our servers straight away and removes you from your friends' leaderboards. If you no longer have the app, email sahilsingh1601@gmail.com and we will remove it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data.

Children

No Scroll is not directed at children under 13.
